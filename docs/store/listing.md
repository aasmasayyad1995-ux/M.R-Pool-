## Full description

```
Mr. Pool 3D is a game of 8 ball pool on a table that behaves like one.

PLAY SOMEBODY REAL
Take on whoever is waiting with Quick Match, or make a private room and read the code
to a friend. You both play the same table, shot for shot.

THREE ROBOTS TO BEAT
The beginner aims roughly and hits too hard. The medium one picks the right ball and
keeps control of the cue ball. The hard one rehearses every shot, clears the table when
it can, and hooks you when it cannot.

A TABLE THAT BEHAVES
Side spin, top spin and draw all do what they should. Balls throw off the line on a thin
cut, cushions take the pace off, and the guide line shows you where the cue ball is
going before you commit to the shot.

EARN YOUR KIT
Twelve cues and twenty tables, every one of them won by playing. A better cue draws a
longer guide line. A different cloth changes how the whole table plays: the heavy cloth
in the local club runs slower than the tournament cloth at the top.

NOTHING IN THIS GAME COSTS MONEY
There are no in-app purchases of any kind. Coins are won at the table, or by choosing to
watch an advert. They cannot be bought, they have no cash value, and they cannot be
exchanged for anything outside the game. This is not a gambling app.

YOUR PICTURE STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
Set a profile picture if you like — it is never uploaded anywhere. An online opponent
sees the name you chose, and nothing else.

WHAT YOU NEED
An internet connection, which the game needs in order to play. Android 7.0 or newer, and
a phone that supports OpenGL ES 3.0 — nearly all of them do.
```

## Why it is written this way

**No comparisons to other games.** Naming a competitor in a listing is the quickest way
to a rejection, and it does not sell anything.

**The money paragraph is there on purpose.** It is the first thing a cautious player
looks for in a game with coins in it, and it is the answer to the gambling question on
the content form. Saying it in the listing, in the privacy policy and on the form, in the
same words, is what makes it believable.

**No keyword stuffing.** Repeating "pool billiards snooker 8 ball free" reads as spam to
a person and is against Play's metadata policy. Everything above is a sentence somebody
would actually say.
